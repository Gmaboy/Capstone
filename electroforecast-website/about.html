Hybrid BLSTM–SARIMA Crowdsourcing Platform for Electricity Consumption Forecasting

A web-based platform that collects electricity consumption data through crowdsourcing and applies a hybrid deep-learning and statistical model to forecast demand and classify consumption levels for GUIMELCO, researchers, and consumers.

About the Project

The Hybrid BLSTM-SARIMA Crowdsourcing Platform for Electricity Consumption Forecasting and Demand Level Classification is a web-based platform developed to collect electricity consumption data through crowdsourcing and utilize advanced machine learning models for forecasting and demand classification. The system integrates Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BLSTM) and Seasonal AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average (SARIMA) algorithms to improve the accuracy of electricity consumption predictions.

The project aims to provide utility companies, researchers, and consumers with a reliable forecasting tool that can analyze historical electricity usage patterns and predict future consumption trends. Through the integration of crowdsourced data, the platform continuously gathers real-time information from users, enhancing the dataset used for forecasting and classification.

The system also classifies electricity demand into Low, Normal, High, and Peak levels, allowing users and energy providers to better understand consumption behavior, optimize energy distribution, and support informed decision-making.

Key Definitions

BLSTM
Bidirectional LSTM used to forecast future residential electricity consumption from sequential data.
SARIMA
Seasonal ARIMA model that captures seasonal patterns in historical consumption data.
Crowdsourcing Platform
Enables participating households to voluntarily submit appliance and consumption data.
Explainable AI (XAI)
Explains how and why the model produces its predictions, using SHAP or LIME.
