Crowdsourcing Platform for Electricity Consumption Forecasting and Demand Level Classification

A web-based platform that collects household appliance data through crowdsourcing, forecasts residential electricity demand for GUIMELCO from its real monthly records, and classifies daily demand levels for utility staff, researchers, and consumers.

About the Project

The monthly energy forecast is trained on GUIMELCO's real residential records for 2020–2025. Several models are compared with a rolling-origin backtest (SARIMA, a hybrid BLSTM–SARIMA that adds a bidirectional LSTM trained on SARIMA's errors, Holt-Winters, regression, and a seasonal-naive baseline), and the most accurate one is used. The annual peak is forecast from energy and historical load factors.

GUIMELCO does not yet provide household, hourly, or feeder data, so daily demand data is synthetic: generated to match the real monthly totals, customer counts, and annual peaks exactly. Every page and table that uses it is labeled "Synthetic".

Daily demand is classified into Low, Medium, and High levels with a random forest, compared against a logistic regression baseline. Crowdsourced appliance submissions are reviewed by administrators before they are used.

Key Definitions

SARIMA
Seasonal ARIMA model that captures trend and yearly seasonality in monthly consumption.
MAPE
Mean absolute percentage error of the forecast on held-out years. Lower is better.
Crowdsourcing Platform
Enables participating households to voluntarily submit appliance and consumption data.
Explainable AI (XAI)
Shows which inputs drive the models, using permutation importance and a trend/seasonality breakdown.
