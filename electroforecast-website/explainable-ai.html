About Explainable AI in this System

This page shows which inputs drive the trained models. The demand classifier is explained with permutation importance: how much its held-out macro-F1 drops when a feature is shuffled. For the selected SARIMA model, the forecast breakdown is a descriptive STL decomposition (trend and seasonality) of history plus forecast, not the model's internal components. The classifier is trained on synthetic data; the energy forecast is trained on real GUIMELCO data. The classifier's importances reflect how the synthetic daily data was constructed (real monthly energy plus daily variation), not observed GUIMELCO behaviour.
