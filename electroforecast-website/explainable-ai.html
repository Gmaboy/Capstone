About Explainable AI in this System

Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) explains how and why the Hybrid BLSTM–SARIMA model produces its forecasts and demand classifications. This platform uses SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to measure each feature's contribution to a prediction, and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations) to approximate model behavior around individual predictions — helping utility companies, researchers, and consumers understand the factors influencing their electricity demand forecasts.
