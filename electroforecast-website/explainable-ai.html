About Explainable AI in this System

This page shows which inputs drive the trained models. The demand classifier is explained with permutation importance: how much its held-out accuracy drops when a feature is shuffled. The monthly energy forecast is explained by splitting each forecast into trend, seasonality, and customer-growth components. The classifier is trained on synthetic data; the energy forecast is trained on real GUIMELCO data.
