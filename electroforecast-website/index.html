About

What GUIMPOWERCORE does

A capstone research platform built with the Guimaras Electric Cooperative (GUIMELCO) to help the utility, researchers, and households understand where residential electricity demand is heading.

Demand forecasting

Monthly energy and annual peak forecasts trained on GUIMELCO's real residential records. Several models are tested on past years and the most accurate one is used.

Demand level classification

Each day is classified as Low, Medium, or High demand so staff can plan ahead and households can shift heavy appliance use away from peak hours.

Crowdsourced household data

Households submit appliance ratings and monthly bills. An administrator reviews every submission before it is used in the dataset.

Explainable results

The platform shows which factors drive each prediction and labels every chart as real GUIMELCO data, crowdsourced, or synthetic.

How to Use

Get started in five steps

For households in Guimaras who want to track their usage and contribute to the study.

  1. 1

    Create a household account

    Sign up with your name, email, municipality, and barangay. It takes about a minute.

  2. 2

    Add your appliances

    Pick appliances from the approved catalog or add your own with the power rating from the nameplate label, plus how many hours a day you use them.

  3. 3

    Submit your monthly bill

    Enter the kWh from your GUIMELCO bill and attach a photo of it so it can be checked.

  4. 4

    Wait for validation

    An administrator reviews each submission. You get a notification when it is approved, or a note explaining what to fix.

  5. 5

    View forecasts and insights

    See your estimated daily load, your monthly usage history, the demand forecast for Guimaras, and which days are expected to be High demand.

GUIMELCO staff and researchers sign in with accounts created by an administrator to access the forecast dashboard, validated data, and reports.
